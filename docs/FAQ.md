# FAQ

**Is there a subscription?**
No. One key, $19, paid once, for all your devices (up to 5). Updates included.

**Why crypto only?**
It keeps ClipSync account‑free and lets you buy without giving anyone your card. USDC is a dollar‑pegged stablecoin; the Base network has fees of a few cents. Any wallet that supports Base (Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet, …) works. Send the **exact** amount shown on the payment page.

**I paid but have no key.**
The page updates by itself once the transfer has 6 confirmations on Base (about a minute). If you closed the page, open the link from your order again, or paste the transaction hash on the payment page. Still nothing after 30 minutes? Write to support with the transaction hash — never your seed phrase.

**Does it work without Google Play / Google services?**
Yes. ClipSync is distributed as an APK from our site, QR scanning is built in, and nothing depends on Google services.

**Does it work over the internet?**
No — by design ClipSync only talks between devices on the same local network. There is no server that sees your data.

**Is it compatible with KDE Connect?**
No. ClipSync uses its own encrypted protocol and focuses on multi‑device clipboard plus Windows and macOS in one app.

**Why does the Android app ask for Accessibility?**
Only for controlling the phone with the mouse and keyboard during screen mirroring, and only if you turn that on. Everything else works without it.

**What does the free tier include?**
Clipboard text and links with one paired device, pairing with a code, and a 7‑day full trial after the first pairing.

**Can I move my license to another device?**
Yes — deactivate the old device in Settings or unlink it on the Activate page of the website, then activate the new one with the same key.

**Where are my files saved?**
In *Downloads/ClipSync* on the computer and in *Download/ClipSync* on the phone. Existing files are never overwritten — a numbered copy is created instead.

**Where is the source code?**
ClipSync is proprietary software; this repository holds the product page and documentation.
